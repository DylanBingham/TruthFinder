Information

Polarity: the overall polarity (sentiment) of an article as a whole. Completely Negative = -1, Completely Positive = 1.

Subjectivity: the overall subjectivity (how much or how little an article expresses a personal viewpoint) of an article as a whole. More Factual Content = 0, More Opinionated Content = 1.

Avg Polarity: the average polarity of the article's sentences. A more fine-grained measure of polarity.

Avg Subjectivity: the average subjectivity of the article's sentences. A more fine-grained measure of subjectivity.

Word Count: the length of an article.

Quotes: an approximation of the number of quotations in an article. Counts number of times "said", "say", "told", and "tell" speech attributes appear in the article.

Flesch Reading Ease: a measure of text readability based on text length and syllabic content. Difficult to Read = 1, Easy to Read = 100.
